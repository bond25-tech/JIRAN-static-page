JIRAN

Connecting Neighbors in Gated Communities

Welcome to Jiran—which means "neighbors" in Arabic—your dedicated community app designed exclusively for gated communities and residential compounds. We believe that great neighborhoods are built on connection, trust, and mutual support. Jiran brings neighbors together through a seamless digital platform that makes community living more convenient, friendly, and collaborative.

Our Vision: To transform gated communities into thriving neighborhoods where residents support each other, share resources, and build lasting connections that make everyday life easier and more enjoyable.

Our Community Services

  • Ride-sharing within your compound—catch a lift with neighbors heading the same way
  • Food ordering and delivery from neighbors—enjoy homemade meals from your community
  • Neighborhood marketplace for buying, selling, or borrowing items locally
  • Community events and activities coordination
  • Emergency assistance and neighborhood watch features
  • Skill-sharing platform—connect with neighbors who can help with various tasks

Why Jiran

Living in a gated community means you're surrounded by neighbors, yet often we don't know each other. Jiran bridges this gap by creating a trusted, secure platform where residents can help one another. Whether you need a ride to the gate, want to order fresh food from a neighbor's kitchen, or simply want to connect with people nearby, Jiran makes it happen safely and efficiently.

  • Verified members-only access for your compound's security
  • Built-in rating and review system for trust and safety
  • Real-time notifications for requests and offers
  • Easy in-app messaging to coordinate with neighbors
  • Community guidelines to ensure respectful interactions
